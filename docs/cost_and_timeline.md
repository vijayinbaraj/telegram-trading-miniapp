# Indicative cost and timeline

Assumption: existing FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL, Redis/Celery and paper exchange are reused.

## Additional Mini App work
- React/TypeScript foundation and Telegram SDK: 2-3 days
- Dashboard and navigation: 2-3 days
- Alerts, exceptions and preferences UI: 2-3 days
- Exchange and safety confirmation UI/API integration: 2-4 days
- Plan/profile integration: 2-3 days
- Authentication, security, QA and deployment: 3-5 days

Indicative additional effort: 13-21 working days, depending on existing APIs and schema.

## Combined bot + Mini App
Indicative end-to-end implementation: approximately 25-40 working days when integrating
the full customer journey, billing, admin, security and production testing.

Self-development has no additional developer fee, but excludes your time and hosting.
External development quotations should follow a code/schema review.

## Operating cost
A small pilot may fit an indicative INR 2,000-10,000/month incremental infrastructure
budget when reusing existing services. Production/high-availability and trading workloads
can cost materially more. Payment gateway fees, exchange fees, taxes, support and legal
costs are excluded. Load-test before promising a user capacity.
